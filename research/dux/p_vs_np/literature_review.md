# Literature Review: P vs NP Problem

## Key Papers

1. **[Exploring the P vs. NP Problem](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.08668v2)**
   - **Summary:** Investigates entropy profiles of P vs NP problems, highlighting deterministic vs. dispersed characteristics of NP-complete problems.
   - **Key Insight:** NP-complete problems exhibit more complex entropy patterns, suggesting structural differences from P problems.

2. **[A Proof of P != NP (New Symmetric Encryption Algorithm)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.05022)**
   - **Summary:** Claims a proof of P != NP via a novel encryption algorithm. Controversial but worth noting for methodological innovation.
   - **Key Insight:** Proposes cryptographic methods to address computational complexity.

3. **[On the Approximation Method and P vs NP](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.03486)**
   - **Summary:** Extends approximation methods to prove super-polynomial lower bounds for Boolean functions.
   - **Key Insight:** Highlights limitations of approximation techniques in resolving P vs NP.

4. **[Detecting Finer Structure in P vs NP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18968)**
   - **Summary:** Introduces the concept of finer structure within P vs NP, suggesting nuanced subclasses of NP-complete problems.
   - **Key Insight:** NP problems may not be monolithic; subclasses could exist with varying complexities.

5. **[The P vs. NP Brief](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.1207)**
   - **Summary:** A concise overview of the P vs NP problem, its history, and unresolved challenges.
   - **Key Insight:** Provides context for why the problem remains open despite extensive research.
